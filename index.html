About

When something goes wrong, you may have no signal, no time and no one to ask.

Survive Offline puts the answer in your pocket before you need it. Tap the emergency, follow the numbered steps, and see what to avoid. The big red button calls your country's emergency number. Everything is on the phone, so it still works in the bush, at sea, in a storm or when the network is down.

Important

Not a replacement for help

Survive Offline gives general first aid and survival information. It is not medical advice. It does not replace emergency services or a first aid course. Always call your local emergency number first when you can.

FAQ

Common questions

Does it really work with no internet?

Yes. Everything is inside the app. It does not even have Android's internet permission.

What does it collect about me?

Nothing. There is no account, no tracking and no ads. Anything you type stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. It is for Android first.

Which countries does it cover?

All of them. Pick your country and the Call button uses your emergency number. The wildlife guide covers eastern Australia in detail plus the most dangerous species worldwide.
