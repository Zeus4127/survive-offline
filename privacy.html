Survive Offline: privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Survive Offline is an offline first aid and survival guide for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app or this website. In short: nothing you do in the app leaves your phone.

No data collection

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet by itself.

Information you enter

You may choose to enter emergency information (such as your name, blood type, allergies, medicines and emergency contacts), save places such as your car or camp, tick off checklists, save cards or note times. This is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. If you have Android's own backup switched on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Location

If you use "Find my way back", the offline map, "My location" or another tool that needs GPS, the app asks for permission to use your phone's location. Your coordinates are shown on screen and, for saved places, kept on your phone only. They are never sent anywhere. You can refuse or remove this permission at any time in Android settings, and the rest of the app still works.

Optional downloads

You can choose to download extra HD photos and trail maps from Settings. Google Play downloads these for the app, under Google Play's own terms and privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing about you.

Camera flash

The torch and SOS tools switch the phone's flash on and off. The app does not take photos or use the camera image.

Sharing and printing

If you tap Share or Print, Android opens its own share or print screen and you choose where the information goes. The app does not send it anywhere by itself.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them. The website host may keep standard server logs.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

Survive Offline gives general first aid and survival information. It is not medical advice and does not replace emergency services or professional training.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. Questions: contact the developer through the email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
